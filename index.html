02 / STRATEGY PROPOSALS

Two formats.
Two participation habits.

THE MASKED SINGER

One clue.
One guess.

“The clue is on screen. Who is behind the mask?”

A 20–30 second edit opens with an unresolved clue, adds captions and invites a prediction. Keep reveal imagery behind a clear spoiler warning.

Hypothesis: clue-first framing increases qualified episode-page visits compared with a performance-first edit from the same episode.

THE FLOOR

Your category.
Your turn.

“Your category: movie titles. How many can you name?”

A 20–30 second category challenge leaves time to answer, then shows the contestant payoff. End with one episode destination and a clear CTA.

Hypothesis: challenge-first framing increases qualified episode-page visits compared with a host-introduction edit from the same episode.

Concept copy is original, illustrative and untested. Actual footage, music and visual prompts require rights clearance. No mocked campaign results appear here.

03 / PROPOSED EXPERIMENT

Measure the next step,
not just the play.

Primary metric

Qualified episode-page visits
per 1,000 served impressions

A qualified visit lasts at least 10 seconds on an owned episode page. Use consented events, variant IDs and a fixed 24-hour window after a tagged click. No tracking runs on this portfolio site.

Decision rule

Randomize variants within each show and episode. Match duration, audience, CTA and budget. Report an adjusted confidence interval for lift. Scale only with a positive result and acceptable guardrails.

Completion rate and click-through rate diagnose the funnel. Spoiler complaints, negative feedback and cost per qualified visit protect quality. If the test is underpowered, report it as inconclusive.

ASSUMPTION, NOT A FORECAST

A 1.0% baseline and 1.2% treatment rate require approximately 52,000 independent impressions per arm, per show, at 80% power with two-sided α = 0.025. Repeat exposure or clustering may require more. Recalculate from real baselines before launch.

The 30-day pilot

  1. 01–07 / Baseline + rights

    Audit 30 recent uploads per show. Separate formats, validate Topics, and confirm footage rights and destination access.

  2. 08–14 / Creative + tracking

    Build matched variants from two episodes per show. QA captions, spoiler treatment, UTMs and consented events.

  3. 15–30 / Test + decision

    Run the prespecified test. Report effect, uncertainty and guardrails. Extend an underpowered test only under the prespecified rule.
