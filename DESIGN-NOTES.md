# Presentation visual reference

The FOX-inspired revision uses the public [OneFOX 2024 presentation](https://media.foxadsolutions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/03120903/OneFOX_2024-1.pdf) as a visual reference for navy, blue and white contrast, strong sans-serif headings and prominent data. Accessed October 19, 2026. No proprietary internal FOX template was available or used. This independent project retains its non-affiliation disclosure. Research content and values remain unchanged.

The cover uses original AI-generated artwork, rather than copied FOX artwork or logos. Built-in ImageGen generated the asset saved as `assets/broadcast-blue.png`. All slide text and charts remain editable in the PowerPoint file.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: productivity-visual. Create an original premium television network strategy presentation BACKGROUND only, 16:9 landscape. Deep midnight navy #001E42 with saturated broadcast blue #0067B9 illuminated sculptural curved surfaces concentrated at the far right edge and lower right corner. Very subtle 3D studio lighting on a large cropped smooth blue arc, tasteful and restrained. Left 70 percent nearly solid dark navy, calm empty negative space for white executive slide text. Upper and lower left especially quiet. Crisp sophisticated corporate broadcast aesthetic. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no people, no charts. This is an original abstract background for an independent portfolio, not official brand artwork.
